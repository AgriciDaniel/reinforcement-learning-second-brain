# Reinforcement Learning Brain Product Spec

## Buyer

ML engineers, researchers, and AI builders who need repeatable, source-cited reinforcement learning decisions, from classic RL through modern LLM post-training.

## Domain

reinforcement learning: fundamentals, deep RL, RLHF/RLAIF and preference optimization, evaluation, tooling, and applied best practices

## Core Workflows

- Source intake and provenance capture for papers, docs, and courses
- Source quality review and claim verification
- Algorithm selection and training-run decision drafting
- RLHF / preference-optimization pipeline review
- Experiment debugging and reproducibility review
- Weekly research-refresh and next-action review

## Deliverables

- Algorithm selection guide
- RLHF / post-training pipeline playbook
- Debugging and reproducibility checklist
- Evaluation and benchmark scorecard
- Weekly research-refresh report

## Promise

Turn raw sources and recurring decisions into a persistent, source-cited
operating brain.

## Non-Promises

- No unsourced claims about algorithm performance, benchmarks, or state of the art
- No credentials, tokens, API keys, or private training data in repo artifacts
- No claims of a universal best algorithm; recommendations must state task assumptions
- No presenting contested or folklore practices as evidence-based without labeling
